Manifesto

Most agencies sell the illusion of growth — impressions, reach, and engagement metrics that look good in a PDF and mean nothing in a P&L. Vajra Social was built to be different. We build the infrastructure. You own the results.

The Problem

"Spend goes up. Profit doesn't."

Most ad accounts are scaled before the funnel is stable, so budget multiplies waste instead of revenue.

"Creative feels busy, not decisive."

Without a testing system, brands confuse content volume with signal and never isolate what actually converts.

"Reporting is noise, not intelligence."

Dashboards become decoration when they stop connecting channel metrics to contribution margin, payback, and cash flow.

The Vajra Method

01

Diagnose the economics.

Before a single campaign goes live, we map acquisition math, contribution margin, payback window, and offer viability so the account is built on commercial truth.

02

Architect the funnel.

Landing flow, tracking, CRM handoff, remarketing logic, and conversion paths are engineered as one system instead of disconnected marketing tasks.

03

Build creative signal.

We develop testing matrices for hooks, formats, offers, and objections so the brand learns faster than the market changes.

04

Scale with discipline.

Budget expansion follows clear thresholds, not impulse. Winning units are extended, weak variables are cut, and channel mix is rebalanced weekly.

05

Report what matters.

Founders see only the numbers that affect decisions: CAC, payback, MER, qualified pipeline, and revenue quality by source.
